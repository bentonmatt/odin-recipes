Lasagna

This recipe includes pasta, eggs, cottage cheese, mozzarella, ground turkey, pasta sauce, and spices.

Ingredients

  • Medium Egg Noodles
  • Marinara
  • Cottage Cheese
  • Eggs
  • Ground Turkey
  • Mozzarella

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in pasta sauce.
  2. Mix cottage cheese, eggs, and spices.
  3. Layer the cooked pasta in bottom of 9x9 pan, and layer cottage cheese mixture on top.
  4. Add layer of cooked ground turkey.
  5. Add layer of pasta on top, and top with mozzarella cheese.
